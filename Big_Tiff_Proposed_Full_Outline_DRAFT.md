# Big Tiff — Proposed Full Novel Outline (Draft for Sammy's Review)

**Prepared by:** Claude, at Aaron's request, synthesizing: the current Master
Story Bible / Character Dossiers / Timeline / World Cosmology, Aaron's rough

---

## How to read this document

Each act lists its scenes/beats in order. For each one:
- **Status** — `DRAFTED` (already written), `OUTLINED` (named in canon/rough
  structure but not yet scened), or `PROPOSED` (suggested here to fill a
  structural gap; needs Sammy's judgment, not assumed).
- **Beat function** — which of the standard story-beat roles it's filling,
  for cross-checking pacing, not as a rule to obey rigidly.
- **Unit type** — `scene` (full dramatized scene, 7-part template) or
  `connector` (shorter bridge: `sequel`, `setup`, or `deepening` — see
  Section 6 for what each means).

## ACT ONE

### 1. Tiff's past intro (age 8)
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Opening Image (the "before")
- **Unit type:** scene
- Brief — establishes Tiff as a child, before loss. Should be short; this is
  scaffolding for the storm, not a full chapter of childhood.

### 2. The first storm — Tiff's parents die
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Inciting wound (pre-story trauma, not the story's own
  Catalyst — that comes later)
- **Unit type:** scene

### 3. Tiff arrives at the Home; first encounter with Timmy
- **Status:** DRAFTED (referenced in Character Dossiers: the locket incident)
- **Beat function:** Theme planted (the locket/outburst moment seeds both
  Tiff's grief and Timmy's unfulfilled goblin assignment)
- **Unit type:** scene

### 4. Five years later — Tiff's daily life at the Home
- **Status:** DRAFTED (4 existing scenes: Morning Wake-Up, Drawing Circle,
  Helping a Friend, Pre-Storm Tension)
- **Beat function:** Setup — establish the "normal world," Tiff's
  competence and warmth, Timmy's resentment, first hints of odd
  occurrences (good luck immediately following bad luck)
- **Unit type:** scene (existing) — consider one `setup` connector if a
  caretaker introduction needs its own short beat rather than folding into
  an existing scene

### 5. PROPOSED: A quiet beat where Tiff almost names her own discomfort
- **Status:** PROPOSED
- **Beat function:** Theme Stated — someone other than Tiff (a caretaker, or
  even Lumi/Thornak in a later echo) voices something adjacent to "balance,
  not good vs. evil" early, so it can be dismissed or misunderstood now and
  understood later.
- **Unit type:** connector (`deepening`)
- This is optional polish, not a structural requirement — flag to Sammy as
  a nice-to-have, not a gap that blocks anything.

### 6. The second storm
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Catalyst
- **Unit type:** scene
- Tiff sees light entities, is led into the ocean, has her NDE. Same storm
  triggers Timmy's escape attempt (parallel cross-cut opportunity — two
  scenes or one scene with a clean POV/section break).

### 7. PROPOSED: Bridge between the storm and Tiff waking
- **Status:** PROPOSED
- **Beat function:** sequel (reaction to the NDE itself, before full
  comprehension sets in)
- **Unit type:** connector (`sequel`)
- A short beat of disorientation/fear immediately after the NDE, before
  scene 8's dawning awareness. Prevents an abrupt jump from "nearly drowned"
  to "calmly observing goblins."

### 8. Tiff awakens — sees the hidden world
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Debate ("what is happening to me / am I broken")
- **Unit type:** scene
- Observes portals/tears, insects mending them. Her own confusion and fear
  here is the Debate beat — she hasn't yet chosen to engage with this new
  reality.

### 9. Meeting the guide pair (Lumi & Thornak)
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Break into Two — the threshold she can't walk back
  across
- **Unit type:** scene
- Reveal that Thornak was meant for Timmy. This is also where the B Story
  (Tiff/Timmy as mirrored, not romantic, parallel arcs) is formally seeded.

## ACT TWO-A (the "tour" / guidebook stretch)

### 10. The tour of the frequency world
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Fun and Games — the section that delivers on the
  story's core promise (what is it like to see this hidden world?)
- **Unit type:** mixed — several short `setup` connectors interspersed with
  full scenes
- Aaron's notes describe this as potentially large, almost guidebook-like.
  **Craft flag for Sammy:** worldbuilding-heavy sections risk reading as
  inert exposition if every beat is pure information delivery. Recommend
  each `setup` connector carry a small friction or problem (a guide
  disagreement, a rule Tiff resists, a glimpse of something wrong) rather
  than neutral world-tour narration — keeps momentum without sacrificing
  the immersive "guidebook" quality Aaron wants.

### 11. Timmy finds his own way into the realm
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** B Story development, running parallel to Tiff's
  training
- **Unit type:** scene
- His exposure should feel different in texture from Tiff's guided tour —
  unsupervised, anxious, possibly adversarial with whatever he encounters,
  reinforcing that he has no guide and is improvising.

### 12. Tiff begins harnessing her ability
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Midpoint approach — building competence before the
  turn
- **Unit type:** scene
- Training scenes with Lumi/Thornak. The Home's nexus location should
  visibly amplify her growth here, tying back to established cosmology.

## ACT TWO-B (rising stakes)

### 13. PROPOSED: The Midpoint — Tiff realizes the threat is bigger than herself
- **Status:** PROPOSED (placement; the content is implied by Aaron's notes
  but not yet pinned to one scene)
- **Beat function:** Midpoint — the turn from external want ("learn to
  control my gift") to internal need ("I have to stop this before it's too
  late")
- **Unit type:** scene
- **Recommend Sammy choose the literal moment** — likely when Tiff first
  perceives the *scale* of Timmy's unbalanced impact on the wider system,
  not just on himself. Naming this scene explicitly (rather than letting
  the turn happen gradually/off-page) gives the back half of the book a
  clear pivot to build toward and away from.

### 14. Timmy's escalating threat to the wider system
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Bad Guys Close In
- **Unit type:** scene(s) — likely more than one
- This is the single most urgent content gap from the original review: the
  logline promises Timmy's "controlling behavior" as the spine of external
  conflict, but it needs concrete, escalating, dramatized incidents here —
  not summary. Recommend Sammy draft 2-3 discrete scenes showing specific
  consequences of the growing imbalance (localized first, then spreading),
  each one raising stakes over the last.

### 15. PROPOSED: A sequel beat — Tiff's dilemma about confronting Timmy
- **Status:** PROPOSED
- **Beat function:** sequel (reaction/dilemma/decision) bridging the
  worsening crisis into the All-Is-Lost low point
- **Unit type:** connector (`sequel`)
- Without this, the outline currently jumps fairly directly from "imbalance
  growing" to "world gets destroyed." A short beat of Tiff weighing what
  she's willing to risk gives the coming low point real emotional weight
  rather than just escalating plot mechanics.

### 16. The world of balance begins to collapse
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** All Is Lost / Dark Night of the Soul
- **Unit type:** scene
- This should be allowed to actually hurt — per the source material, the
  low point is strongest when it's unambiguous, not softened. Recommend
  protecting its placement so it isn't undercut by being rushed too close
  to the climax.

## ACT THREE

### 17. The confrontation
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Climax, part 1 (conflict)
- **Unit type:** scene
- Tiff and Timmy face each other directly. This should resolve as a
  **turn**, not a defeat — the outline specifies they become teammates, not
  that one bests the other.

### 18. Resolving Timmy's original imbalance
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Climax, part 2 (resolution of the core wound — the
  unfulfilled goblin assignment from five years ago)
- **Unit type:** scene
- Mirrored internal balancing in both characters. Recommend this be the
  scene where their alliance is forged, immediately following the
  confrontation rather than as a separate later beat — keeps the
  enemies-to-teammates turn tight and earned rather than diffuse.
- Per Aaron's confirmation: this teaming-up moment is also where a
  **romantic hint may first surface** — light, not declared. The two becoming
  allies-in-arms is the justification for the hint; it should read as
  *possible*, not confirmed, leaving real room for the epilogue to do the
  fuller reveal work.

### 19. The guide pair's purpose fulfilled
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Climax, part 3 (subplot resolution)
- **Unit type:** scene
- Lumi and Thornak disappear from the immediate scene, their roles
  complete. Timmy gains chaos-frequency control; Tiff gains command over
  both — a clean power-asymmetry payoff that also visually marks her as the
  more "complete" balance-keeper, fitting her protagonist role.

### 20. Bringing balance back — physical and other realms
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Resolution (practical/world-level)
- **Unit type:** scene
- Tiff and Timmy restore the physical realm together; the Faery and Goblin
  guides (separately) restore their own realms. Good opportunity for a
  brief multi-thread sequence rather than one long scene — three small
  resolutions in parallel.

### 21. The guides' fates — karmic evolution
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Falling action / B-thread closure
- **Unit type:** scene or connector (`deepening`) — likely short

### 22. Tiff and Timmy's fates — union, implied
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Falling action / emotional resolution
- **Unit type:** scene

### 23. The Home's fate
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Falling action / thematic payoff
- **Unit type:** scene
- The other children benefit from the restored balance. The asymmetry
  detail (only Tiff sees both creatures; only Timmy sees goblins) is a
  strong, concrete final-image-adjacent image — recommend protecting this

### 24. The bureaucracy returns — hint of a bigger problem
- **Status:** OUTLINED
- **Beat function:** Sequel hook (sets up book two)
- **Unit type:** scene or connector (`sequel`)

## Section 6: Connector Unit Types (for schema discussion)

For Sammy's awareness — these are being proposed as a possible addition to
how the webtool structures scenes, not yet built:

- **`sequel`** — a short beat where a character reacts to what just
  happened, weighs a dilemma, and reaches a decision. Bridges a scene's
  unresolved ending into the next scene's goal.
- **`setup`** — minimal necessary background/context delivery, kept short
  and given a small friction point so it doesn't read as inert exposition.
- **`deepening`** — a brief, non-essential-to-plot enrichment of character
  or world feeling; used sparingly.
- **`ellipsis`** — an explicit, deliberate time/location jump where nothing
  is bridged; a hard cut is the correct choice, not something to soften.

These would be optional, Sammy-judged additions to the existing scene
schema — never auto-generated by the tool itself, consistent with how
review-status and `needsReviewReason` already work (Sammy's editorial
judgment is the source of truth; the tool only renders and persists it).
